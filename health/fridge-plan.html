Post-workout shake

At home, within 30–60 min of training
1 scoop Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard whey
250 ml skim or light milk (or water)
½ cup blueberries
≈ 260 kcal · 37 g protein
It replaces something. Skip the 3:30 pm snack that day. After a 7 pm session, skip the milk + collagen.

Lunch options

~35–45 g protein · 450–600 kcal
  1. Roast chicken bowl: 150 g BBQ chicken (no skin) + ½ rice pouch + salad kit
  2. Tuna rice bowl: 185 g tin tuna + ½ rice pouch + cucumber, tomato, lemon
  3. Egg & chickpea curry: 3 eggs + ½ tin chickpeas in masala + 1 chapati
  4. Chicken wrap: wholemeal wrap + 120 g chicken + salad + yogurt-mint
  5. Salmon tray bake: salmon + broccoli + baby potatoes, 20 min at 200°C
  6. Paneer or tofu bhurji: 150 g + onion, tomato + 1 chapati
  7. Dal & chicken meal prep: dal + grilled chicken thigh

Dinner options

~30–40 g protein · 500–650 kcal · + vegetables
  1. Chicken curry: 150 g chicken + 2 chapatis + cucumber-onion salad
  2. Fish curry: 150 g basa, barra or ling + ⅔ cup rice + poriyal
  3. Dal + paneer tikka: 1 cup dal + 100 g paneer + 2 chapatis + spinach
  4. Egg curry: 3 eggs in light masala + 2 chapatis + veg
  5. Stir-fry: 150 g chicken or tofu + frozen veg + ⅔ cup rice
  6. Kheema with peas: 150 g lean mince + 2 chapatis
  7. Sambar + grilled chicken: + 1 dosa or ⅔ cup rice
  8. Salmon + roast veg: pumpkin, zucchini, capsicum
Portions
½ a 250 g rice pouch ≈ ⅔ cup ≈ 190 kcal. Use half a salad-kit dressing.
Shopping staples
Eggs, tinned tuna and salmon, rice pouches, salad kits, frozen veg, Greek yogurt, curd.
Curry tips
Thicken with tomato, onion or yogurt, not cream. 1 tsp oil for the tadka.
Track progress
• Weigh in 3–4 mornings a week and compare the weekly average
• Measure your waist every 2 weeks (target: under 90 cm)
• Aim for ~0.5 kg a week. Slower is fine when you're building muscle
