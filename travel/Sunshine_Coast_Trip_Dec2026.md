## 7. Kayaking: What to Know

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Kayaking experience needed?** | No. Both outings are beginner-friendly, with stable kayaks (often doubles) and a briefing or lesson. |
| **Must we be able to swim?** | Usually not required, but you should be comfortable in water. Life jackets are provided and must be worn. **Tell the operator about any non-swimmers when booking.** |
| **Fitness?** | Moderate. Several hours of steady, easy paddling with breaks on Thursday. Expect sore shoulders the next day. |
| **Conditions?** | The Everglades narrows are flat and sheltered. Lake Cootharaba can get choppy later in the day, which is why tours start early. Guides reschedule if the weather is poor. |

### The Two Paddles Compared
| | **Thu – Everglades Tour** | **Sat – River Hire** |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Guided, full day | Self-guided, 1–2 hrs |
| Where | Upper Noosa River, Cooloola (Great Sandy National Park) | Lower Noosa River and Noosa Sound, in town |
| Feel | Remote wilderness, mirror-like water | Relaxed paddle past waterfront homes and sandbanks |
| Booking | Essential, book now | Advisable, a few days ahead |
| Optional? | No, it's the highlight | Yes. Drop it if one paddle is enough. |

## 8. What to Wear & Pack

### Kayaking
- Quick-dry shorts and top (avoid cotton)
- Long-sleeved sun shirt or rash shirt
- Water shoes or sports sandals with a back strap (no thongs)
- Wide-brim hat with chin strap; sunglasses with strap
- Swimmers underneath
- Change of clothes and a towel to leave in the car
- Small dry bag for phone, keys and wallet

### Walking
- Walking or light hiking shoes with good grip (Ngungun and Coolum are rocky and steep)
- Light, sweat-wicking shirts and shorts
- Hat, sunglasses, small daypack
- Water: **at least 1.5 L per person** per walk
- Swimmers for Kondalilla and Tea Tree Bay
- Light layer for the 4am Mt Coolum start

### Evenings
- Smart-casual: shorts or chinos with a collared shirt or nice top, sandals or casual shoes

### Essentials
- Light rain jacket or packable poncho
- Sunscreen SPF 50+ (reef-safe), lip balm with SPF
- Insect repellent (a must in the Everglades and on Mt Coolum)
- Beach towel (check whether the resort provides them)

## 9. Handy Tips
- **Start every walk early.** It's hot by mid-morning, and storms often build in the afternoon.
- **Thursday is the anchor day.** If the weather cancels the Everglades tour, swap it with Friday or Saturday. The walks are easy to reshuffle (note that Kanu Kapers also runs on Saturdays).
- **Check the Queensland Parks website** the day before each walk for closures.
- **Saturdays in Noosa are busy.** Arrive early for parking and book dinner.
- **Bring plenty of water.** There's no water on the Ngungun or Coolum tracks.
