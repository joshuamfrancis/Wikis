# South East Queensland Road Trip — 27–31 Dec 2026

**Route:** Brisbane → Stanthorpe (Granite Belt) → Tamborine Mountain (Scenic Rim) → Brisbane
**Party:** 4 adults
**Duration:** 5 days / 4 nights
**Total driving:** ~700 km
**Theme:** Nature trails and trekking. No swimming, water sports, theme parks or camping.
**Accommodation source:** Booking.com and Airbnb, prices checked 12 Sep 2026, AUD, totals for the stay (Airbnb totals include cleaning and service fees).

---

## Why this route

Two completely different landscapes in one loop. Girraween in the Granite Belt sits at roughly 900 m — the coolest, driest hiking country in South East Queensland and the best bet for late-December heat. Lamington and Springbrook deliver subtropical rainforest, waterfalls and escarpment lookouts. Main Range at Cunningham's Gap fills the gap between them on a travel day.

---

## Itinerary

### Day 1 — Sunday 27 Dec: Brisbane → Stanthorpe

- Depart early. ~3 hours via the Cunningham Highway, break at Warwick.
- **Afternoon walk:** Mount Marlay lookout (in town, short) or Storm King Dam.
- **Optional:** Granite Belt cellar doors — Ballandean Estate, Golden Grove. Most open between Christmas and New Year.
- Stock up on groceries and water in Stanthorpe. There is no shop at Girraween.

### Day 2 — Monday 28 Dec: Girraween National Park

The big hiking day. Park is ~40 minutes south of Stanthorpe. **On the track by 7:00 am.**

| Walk | Distance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Pyramid | 3 km return | The famous bare granite slab climb. Steep, no shade, ~1.5 h |
| Granite Arch | 1.6 km return | Easy, shaded, good cool-down |
| Castle Rock | 5.2 km return | Granite summit, big views |
| Mt Norman | 11 km return | Extension for a fit group |

Carry 3 L of water per person. No drinking water on the tracks.

### Day 3 — Tuesday 29 Dec: Stanthorpe → Tamborine Mountain

~3.5 hours including the walk stop.

- **Mid-morning:** Main Range National Park at Cunningham's Gap. **Mt Cordeaux** — 6.8 km return to the lookout, or 12 km if you continue to **Bare Rock**. One of the best escarpment walks in the state.
- Continue via Boonah and Beaudesert to Tamborine Mountain.
- **Afternoon:** Curtis Falls (1.5 km) or the Tamborine Rainforest Skywalk.

### Day 4 — Wednesday 30 Dec: Lamington National Park

Full day. Two entrances, ~2 hours apart by road — **pick one**.

**Green Mountains (O'Reilly's)**
- Tree Top Walk
- Box Forest Circuit — 10.9 km, three waterfalls
- Python Rock — 3.1 km
- Morans Falls — 4.4 km
- *Access road is 35 km of narrow winding bitumen. Allow a full hour from Canungra.*

**Binna Burra**
- Coomera Circuit — 17.4 km, ~6 h. The standout hike in South East Queensland.
- Caves Circuit — 5 km, shorter alternative

### Day 5 — Thursday 31 Dec: Springbrook → Brisbane

~1 hour down to Springbrook, ~1.5 hours home from there.

- Purling Brook Falls circuit — 6 km
- Twin Falls Circuit — 4 km
- Best of All Lookout — short
- Natural Bridge — on the way out
- Back in Brisbane late afternoon for New Year's Eve.

## Practical notes

**Weather.** Late December means 30–34 °C, high humidity, extreme UV and frequent afternoon thunderstorms. Start every walk at 6–7 am and be off exposed granite by 11 am.

**Track conditions.** Rainforest tracks at Lamington and Springbrook carry leeches and ticks after rain. Gaiters or long socks help. Check park alerts at parks.desi.qld.gov.au a day or two before each hike — storm closures are common in summer.

**Roads.** Binna Burra Road and O'Reilly's Road are both narrow, winding and slow, with no fuel at the top. Fill up in Canungra.

**Booking.** 27–31 Dec is the highest-demand week of the year in both areas. There are far fewer genuine 4-adult properties in the Granite Belt than on Tamborine, so **book the Stanthorpe leg first** and treat the Tamborine nights as the flexible half. Check cancellation terms on the specific room type — many small operators run stricter policies over the Christmas–New Year block.

**Gear.** 3 L water per person per day, sun protection, first aid, offline maps (patchy reception at Girraween and in the Lamington valleys), sturdy boots with grip for wet granite and rainforest steps.
